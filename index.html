The Origin

Forged in Truth

"It was born from hardship, hunger, cold nights, doubt — and faith."

It all began in January 2024. A wish was made — not to man, not to angels, but to God. It was made by a lonely forklift driver who had given his life to his wife and his children. He never asked for much from the world. He only wanted to make sure his family's needs were met.

In twelve months of chronic pain, a crumbling marriage, and rejection after rejection, he created a universe. Seventeen books. Four feature scripts. A pilot episode. Eighteen merchandise items. And the blueprint for a restaurant that would feed the forgotten — the House of Mercy.

On January 23rd, 2026, he walked 30 kilometres in 30-degree heat to hand-deliver his proposal to OpenAI and Google in Sydney. No suit. Just track pants, a t-shirt, and faith. Because if stories can move hearts — they can also move lives.
