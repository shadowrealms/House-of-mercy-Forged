The Origin

Forged in Truth

"It was born from hardship, hunger, cold nights, doubt — and faith."

It all began in January 2024. A wish was made — not to man, not to angels, but to God. It was made by a lonely forklift driver who had given his life to his wife and his children. He never asked for much from the world. He only wanted to make sure his family's needs were met.

In twelve months of chronic pain, a crumbling marriage, and rejection after rejection, he created a universe. Seventeen books. Four feature scripts. A pilot episode. Eighteen merchandise items. And the blueprint for a restaurant that would feed the forgotten — the House of Mercy.

On January 23rd, 2026, he walked 30 kilometres in 30-degree heat to hand-deliver his proposal to OpenAI and Google in Sydney. No suit. Just track pants, a t-shirt, and faith. Because if stories can move hearts — they can also move lives.

Meet the Guardians

🕊️
Mercy
Compassion in Motion

Mercy steps into the darkest rooms without fear, hearing what the world ignores and holding fast when broken souls begin to shake apart. She reminds the fallen that relapse does not revoke worth, that grief does not disqualify love, and that even in the longest night, no soul is beyond the reach of home.

🌿
Grace
Restoration & Healing

Grace does not rush healing — she makes space for it. She brings warmth to the body before asking the heart to rise, softening rooms shaped by fear, feeding the hungry, steadying the ashamed, and teaching the wounded that restoration is not force, but light returning gently to what has been hidden too long.

🕯️
Mika
Courage Through Darkness

Mika carries the gravity of sorrow without surrendering to it. She brings rhythm back to chaos, steadiness back to trembling bodies, and truth back to minds haunted by grief and fear. Even where death, addiction, and memory try to speak the loudest, she stands as witness that darkness never has the final word.

🌅
Hope
The Hand That Helps Souls Stay

Hope arrives quietly, but never weakly. She brings nourishment before display, patience before pressure, and presence before answers. Where bodies tremble, songs crack, and lives feel too far gone to begin again, Hope kneels in the soil beside them and helps new life take root.

They do not heal the world by force. They heal it by staying when others leave, by holding what others fear, and by carrying hope through rooms where night once believed it would win.
